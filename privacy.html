Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 18, 2026

What is Analytics Agent?

Analytics Agent is an open-source command-line tool that lets users query their own Google Analytics 4, Firebase Authentication, and PostgreSQL data for analysis. The source code is publicly available on GitHub.

What data does the app access?

When you sign in with Google, the app requests the following permissions:

  • Google Analytics (read-only) — to list your GA4 properties and run reports on the property you select.
  • Firebase (read-only) — to list your Firebase projects and read authentication user records.
  • Email address — to identify your account during sign-in.

How is the data used?

All data is used solely to generate analytics reports for you, the authenticated user. The app runs entirely on your own computer — no data is sent to any third-party server.

What the app does NOT do

  • Does not store your Google Analytics or Firebase data on any external server.
  • Does not modify, delete, or write any data to your Google Analytics or Firebase accounts.
  • Does not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties.
  • Does not collect usage analytics or telemetry.

Where are credentials stored?

OAuth2 tokens are stored locally on your machine at ~/.analytics-agent/tokens.json with restricted file permissions (owner-only read/write). You can delete them at any time by running analytics-agent auth logout.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions and removing "Analytics Agent".

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
